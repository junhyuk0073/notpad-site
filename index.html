NotPad Support

NotPad is a note app that works only on your device, with no account. It opens text and formats such as JSON and CSV, and lets you change just the part you pick, compare it, and apply it.

Need help?

Please open an issue on GitHub with your problem or question. Include your device (iPhone or Android), the OS version, and what happened. Issues are public, so please don't post note contents or personal information.
